FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are the tools free?

Yes. The tools are designed to be free to use without creating an account.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Most tools run directly in your browser.

Does ToolNest store my text?

Most text calculations happen locally in your browser. The QR generator uses a third-party QR service to create the image, so do not enter sensitive information into that tool.

How does ToolNest support itself?

The site may use advertising or other clearly disclosed monetization methods when the relevant requirements are met.
